# Assessment Activity Features

The Assessment format is built for formal evaluations, exams, and structured submissions.

## 🕒 Phase-Based Workflow
Assessments are governed by time-bound phases to ensure fairness.

### 1. Reading Phase
- **Goal**: Allow students to review materials without beginning the work.
- **Rules**: Students can view all NoteCards and instructions, but are blocked from creating new notes or editing existing ones.

### 2. Active Phase
- **Goal**: The "Writing" portion of the assessment.
- **Rules**: Students have full permissions to create and edit their assigned notes. A global timer is often displayed.

### 3. Finished Phase
- **Goal**: Lock-in submissions.
- **Rules**: All editing permissions are revoked. The board becomes "Read-Only" for students.

## 📝 Student Submission Logic
- **Assigned Notes**: Features for assigning specific notes or sections to specific students.
- **Progress Tracking**: Real-time indicators for teachers to see who has started and who has finished.

## 🍎 Teacher Grading & Feedback
- **Grading Interface**: A dedicated view for teachers to review student work.
- **Private Feedback**: Teachers can leave comments or scores that are only visible to the specific student.
- **Gradebook Integration**: Scores can be exported or saved to the central `grades` table.

## 🛡️ Integrity Features
- **Time-Locking**: Automatic transition between phases based on the teacher's clock.
- **Edit Tracking**: History of who edited which note and when.
